# AI4Youth Media LTT — Training in Artificial Intelligence for Media Professionals

#### Participated in the AI4Youth Media LTT. A three-day Erasmus+ transnational training held in Belgrade, Serbia and organized by Radio-Television of Serbia (RTS) in partnership with PBS Malta, and Eduheart Prague.

#### The program was led by @Mark Egan, an EBU trainer with BBC experience — and his approach to learning made the content both rigorous and immediately applicable.

The training covered:
* Large language models and prompting techniques
* Generative AI ethics, bias, and responsible use
* AI-supported research and investigation
* Visual and audio content production with AI
* Multimedia storytelling and automated workflows

Beyond the sessions, we worked in groups on hands-on projects:
- Custom GPT: Designed a purpose-built AI assistants by using Gemini Gems.
- Zapier automation agent: An agent that automatically collects and delivers the most important AI developments every morning at 8:00 AM.
- Presentation: A new YouTube video format aimed at covering news, issues and current affairs for 18-25 year olds.
- Interactive map application: Built and published using Lovable: [secret-layers-map.lovable.app](https://secret-layers-map.lovable.app/ )

#### This experience reinforced something I think about a lot in my PhD research: AI tools are only as trustworthy as the systems behind them. That question of verifiability — whether an AI agent is actually doing what it claims to be doing?

The AI4Youth Media project: [ai4youthmedia.eu](https://ai4youthmedia.eu/)
